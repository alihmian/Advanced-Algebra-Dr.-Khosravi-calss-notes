- Status: **pending**.

### L06-Q02 — equality versus isomorphism in the submodule theorem

- Status: **pending**.
- Source variant: `primary`, `sources/lectures/06 simple and semisimple modules.pdf`.
- PDF page/location: 4, bottom theorem, continued by proof on page 5.
- Literal statement: if `M = Σ_{i∈I} M_i` with simple M_i and `N ≤ M`, there is `J ⊆ I` with `N = ⊕_{j∈J} M_j`.
- The proof on page 5 instead concludes `N ≅ M/(⊕_{i∈J₁} M_i) ≅ ⊕_{i∈J₀∖J₁} M_i`.
- Possible readings: literal equality with a subfamily of the given submodules; isomorphism to such a direct sum.
- Consequence: the equality statement is stronger than the proof and fails for e.g. a diagonal submodule of a sum of two isomorphic simple modules. No counterexample or correction is added to the slides as source content.
- Evidence: `docs/ambiguities/lecture06/images/submodule-statement.png` and `submodule-proof.png` (300 dpi crops), compared with the full page 5 proof.
- Question: Should the theorem conclusion be `N ≅ ⊕_{j∈J} M_j`, in agreement with its proof, rather than literal equality?
- User answer: pending.
- Resolution/affected frames: `lec06:submodule-decomposition-pending`, `lec06:submodule-decomposition-proof`; literal statement is visibly unresolved, proof conclusion retains the source's isomorphism symbols.
