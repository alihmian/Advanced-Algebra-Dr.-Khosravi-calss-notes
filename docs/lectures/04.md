### L04-Q01: unitary action in the equality of cyclic submodules

- Status: pending.
- Source: `primary`, `sources/lectures/04 Module.pdf`, page 5 bottom and page 6 top.
- Fragment: after defining a general module, the source says that `Ra` and
  `<a> = {ra + na}` agree if the ring has an identity. It does not explicitly
  assume `1m = m` at this point. The zero action was allowed on page 2.
- Possible readings: all modules in this passage are implicitly unitary; or the
  assertion is meant literally for any module over a unital ring.
- Consequence: the latter is false for a nonzero module with zero action.
- Question: should the equality explicitly assume that M is unitary?
- Evidence: `build/source-pages/lecture-04/primary/identity-assumption.png`.
- User answer: none. Status remains open.
- Working edit, 2026-10-25: `lec04:ra-versus-generated` explicitly labels the
  unitary assumption and equality as a provisional reading.

### L04-Q03: notation for the quotient isomorphism

- Status: pending.
- Source: `primary`, `sources/lectures/04 Module.pdf`, page 7 middle, converse proof.
- Fragment: `phi: R/I -> M`, followed by `r+I -> phi(r)`.

### L04-Q04: direct-sum criterion and intersection notation

- Status: pending.
- Source: `primary`, `sources/lectures/04 Module.pdf`, page 9 middle and
  page 10 corollary (proof continues through page 11).
- Fragments: page 9 writes `M_i intersect sum m_j = {0}`; page 10 asserts
  `M = direct-sum M_i` iff `M_i intersect sum_(j != i) M_j = {0}`, without
  the spanning assumption `M = sum M_i`.
- Possible readings: lowercase m_j means submodules M_j, and spanning is
  implicit; or the criterion is intended only for the sum of the M_i.
- Consequence: independence alone does not say that the submodules span M.
- Question: should the sum use M_j and the criterion explicitly assume
  `M = sum M_i`, or should its conclusion be restricted to `sum M_i`?
- Evidence: `build/source-pages/lecture-04/primary/kernel-intersection.png`
  and `direct-sum-criterion.png` in the same directory.
- User answer: none. Status remains open.
- Working edit, 2026-10-25: `lec04:kernel-uniqueness` uses submodules M_j;
  `direct-sum-criterion`, `direct-sum-proof-intersection`, and
  `direct-sum-proof-uniqueness` state/prove the criterion with the explicit
  provisional spanning hypothesis. All affected frames carry Q04 markers.
