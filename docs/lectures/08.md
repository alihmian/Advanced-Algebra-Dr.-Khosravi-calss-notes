### L08-Q01: Missing second inverse identity

- Status: **open, deferred to the questions PDF for later review**;
  recorded/asked 2026-10-25.
- Source: `primary`, `sources/lectures/08  direct sum.pdf`, PDF page 5,
  top of page above the horizontal rule; map construction on page 4 below.
- Evidence: [crop](../ambiguities/lecture08/images/reverse-proof-gap.png),
  inspected at 250 dpi with surrounding pages 3–5.
- Literal argument: `phi lambda_i = f_i`, `psi f_i = lambda_i` imply
  `psi phi = id_M` by uniqueness; the source then claims that `psi` is invertible.
- Why it matters: a single one-sided inverse does not establish an isomorphism.
- Proposed completion, not yet accepted: for `X = direct sum M_i`, use
  `(phi psi) f_i = f_i` and uniqueness for `X` to derive `phi psi = id_X`.
  Alternative: retain the source argument as explicitly incomplete.
- Concrete question: may the proposed second identity and its uniqueness
  justification be added to complete the proof, or is a different intended
  argument required?

- Resulting action: retained the question, evidence, and proposed completion in
  the questions PDF and added this reply there. This is an instruction to keep
