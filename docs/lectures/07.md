### L07-Q01: domains of the projection maps

- Status: pending.
- Source: `primary`, `sources/lectures/07 Hom.pdf`, PDF page 4, lower half,
  definition of the two projections in the proof of part (i).
- Literal reading: `pi_i: Hom_R(N,M_1) direct-sum Hom_R(N,M_2) -> Hom_R(N,M_i)`.
  Immediately above, the proof uses `psi(f)=(pi_1 compose f, pi_2 compose f)`
  for `f:N -> M_1 direct-sum M_2`.
- Possible readings: the displayed Hom domains/codomains are a source typo and
  the intended projections are `pi_i:M_1 direct-sum M_2 -> M_i`; or the author
  intends other maps/compositions that need to be specified.
- Consequence: the displayed projections cannot compose with the stated `f`.
- Question: may the slides use the coordinate projections
  `pi_1(m_1,m_2)=m_1`, `pi_2(m_1,m_2)=m_2` from `M_1 direct-sum M_2`?
- Evidence: `docs/ambiguities/lecture07/images/projections.png`, rendered at
  260 dpi after inspecting the full page and pages 5-6.
