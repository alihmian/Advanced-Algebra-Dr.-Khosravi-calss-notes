## Source questions recorded during audit (2026-10-25)

### L12-Q01: degree zero and left ideals in Hilbert's proof

- Status: open; provisional reading recorded, not awaiting an answer to finish the pass.
- Source: `primary`, `sources/lectures/12 Noetherian and Artinian rings.pdf`, PDF p. 2 lower half (definition and comparison lemma), p. 3 upper half (degree reduction), p. 4 middle (remaining low degrees), p. 5 upper half (conclusion).
- Observed: definition uses `n in N`, but p. 4 lists only phi_1 through phi_(k-1). Degree zero is not separately treated.
- Possible readings: N includes zero and phi_0 was omitted in the low-degree list; or positive indices are intended, leaving the comparison lemma's constant case unproved. The proof also calls the ideals simply ideals although the conclusion is left Noetherian.
- Consequence: positive-degree leading coefficient data alone does not establish equality of the ideals; a constant remainder must be covered. The chain must cover left ideals to establish left Noetherianity.
- Concrete question: define phi_n for n >= 0 with phi_0(I)=I intersect R, include phi_0 among the low degrees and the constant reduction step, and read all ideals as left ideals?
- Evidence: `docs/ambiguities/lecture12/images/L12-Q01-definition.png` and `L12-Q01-low-degrees.png`.
- User answer: none supplied. Working reading: n >= 0, phi_0(I)=I intersect R, left ideals; include the phi_0 stopping point and m >= ell, with m=ell when k=0. All are marked provisional, not user-confirmed. Affected frames: `lec12:hilbert-statement` through `lec12:hilbert-conclusion`.

### L12-Q02: Hom is used as an R-module

- Status: open; provisional reading recorded, not awaiting an answer to finish the pass.
- Source: `primary`, `sources/lectures/12 Noetherian and Artinian rings.pdf`, p. 11 middle, after off-diagonal Hom decomposition.
- Observed: source identifies a Z-module isomorphism, then says the zero Z-module is also zero as an R-module.
- Possible readings: only the additive-group statement is intended; or an unspecified R-action is intended.
- Consequence: for noncommutative R, Hom_R(U,V) need not have the pointwise R-module structure. Additive-group vanishing is sufficient for the block argument.
- Concrete question: omit the R-module assertion and retain the additive-group/Z-module vanishing, or specify the intended action?
- Evidence: `docs/ambiguities/lecture12/images/L12-Q02-module-structure.png`.
- User answer: none supplied. Working reading: retain additive-group/Z-module vanishing and explicitly flag the omitted R-module inference as provisional in `lec12:off-diagonal-vanishing`; downstream `lec12:block-product` and `lec12:artin-wedderburn-conclusion` retain the dependency.
